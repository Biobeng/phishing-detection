Pages
Deep check: Random Forest, XGBoost and Logistic Regression on what the page contains
Emails
Random Forest, XGBoost and Naive Bayes, soft vote
Cut points
Set on held-out data the models never trained on
Trained on
PhishTank, Tranco, Kaggle MUD, PhiUSIIL, Nazario, SpamAssassin, Enron

Nothing scanned yet

Paste a link or an email and run a scan. You'll get a score from 0 to 100 for how strongly it reads as phishing, cut into three bands so a borderline case comes back as genuinely unsure, not forced into a yes or a no.

Underneath the score you'll also see what each model found on its own, since they don't always agree.
